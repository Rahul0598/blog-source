Methods

A cycle is warm-cold-warm or cold-warm-cold, with the threshold met on both temperature changes. Intensity is the smaller of the two absolute changes: 71 → 27 → 76°F changes by 44°F, then 49°F, so its intensity is 44°F. This ensures both changes are substantial. Overlapping candidates are consolidated, strongest first.

Full-cycle span is the elapsed time from the first temperature to the final temperature, including both changes. Four days cooling followed by five days warming spans nine days. The median summarizes all detected cycles in the record under the current settings.

R² is the fraction of variation between annual values explained by the fitted curve. Linear trends are solid when their 95% change interval excludes zero, dashed otherwise.

The default is a 27°F (15°C) minimum change in each direction.
