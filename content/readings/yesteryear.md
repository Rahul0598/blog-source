I have *GOT* to stop reading books recommended by random people on the internet. This was one was recommended by New York Times, I think - or at least somewhere online. I don't think reading this book was an active decision I made, I saw it mentioned and placed a hold at my local library. All this to say, I wouldn't recommend it to anyone! Why Anne Hathaway agreed to star in its adaptation is beyond me!

I am guessing it was supposed to be a satire, but this was way too long, and arduous to read. The main protagonist was such an infuriating character. The whole book is pure, unadulterated ragebait.
