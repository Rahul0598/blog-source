This book transported me to Italy! I read it with great interest thinking through two-thirds of the book. But the ending wasn't fun. I found it was a slog to finish the last 100 or so pages. 10/10 for the environment of the book, tid bits of comedy, but otherwise I don't know if I would recommend it to anyone else.
